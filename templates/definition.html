What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning is a field of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to learn from data instead of following a fixed set of instructions for every situation.

01

AI in practice

Machine Learning gives AI systems the ability to recognize patterns, make predictions, and support decisions in real-world tasks.

02

Learning from data

Models are trained with examples to find relationships between inputs and outcomes, then use what they learned on new information.

03

Improving models

By evaluating results and refining parameters, a model can improve its performance as it encounters more relevant data.
